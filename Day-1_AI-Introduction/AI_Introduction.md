# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## Examples

- Chatbots
- Voice assistants
- Face recognition
- Recommendation systems
- Self-driving technology

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where computers learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns.
