# Real-World Applications of AI

## 1. Healthcare

AI can help analyze medical images and assist healthcare professionals.

## 2. Education

AI can provide personalized learning and educational assistance.

## 3. Banking

AI can help detect unusual transactions and identify potential fraud.

## 4. Transportation

AI is used in navigation, traffic prediction and driver-assistance systems.

## 5. E-commerce

AI can recommend products based on user activity.

## 6. Chatbots

AI-powered chatbots can answer questions and assist users.
